Google "Code Comprehension" Interview — free practice prompt (shashikant.ai)

Source: Business Insider, Oct 20, 2026 — Google is piloting a code comprehension round where candidates can use Gemini: https://www.businessinsider.com/google-interview-process-ai-gemini-coding-comprehension-software-engineer-2026-10

1. Pick a real codebase file

Tool: GitHub (any open-source repo, e.g. Flask → src/flask/app.py). Open the file, click Raw, copy all of it.

2. Turn ChatGPT into your Google interviewer

Tool: ChatGPT (free plan works). Paste this prompt, then paste the code under it.

Act as a Google interviewer. I will paste a codebase. Ask me one question at a time on architecture, security and performance. Don't give answers. Score me out of 10.

3. Do the 3 tasks of the real round

Tool: same ChatGPT chat. After the questions, paste this:

Now give me 3 tasks on this same code, one at a time: one bug to fix, one small feature to add, and one slow part to make fast. Let me use AI for help, but ask me to explain every change. Score each task out of 10.

4. Keep DSA ready too

The other coding round is still classic DSA with no AI allowed. Practice both.

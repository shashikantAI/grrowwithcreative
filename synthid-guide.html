Photo asli ya AI? Google ka free CHECK

@shashikant.ai · comment CHECK guide · tested on 8 Oct 2026

1. Link

synthid.com

Google ka SynthID Detector: 7 Oct 2026 se sabke liye free (English). Login: Google, OpenAI ya Apple account.

2. Check kaise karein (3 step)

  1. synthid.com kholo → sign in.
  2. Photo, video ya audio upload karo.
  3. 10–40 second "Detecting…" → result.

3. Result ka matlab

ResultMatlab
SynthID was detected
made or edited with Google AI / OpenAI		AI Partner AI ka nishaan mila → AI se bani ya AI se edit hui
SynthID was not detected
unlikely made with AI from a SynthID partner		Partner AI ka nishaan nahi mila. Iska matlab asli NAHI.

4. Kaun pakda jaata hai

  • Google: Gemini, Imagen, Veo, Lyria
  • Partners: OpenAI (ChatGPT), NVIDIA, Kakao · Apple "jald"
  • 180 billion (18,000 crore)+ photos aur videos pe ye nishaan lag chuka hai.

6. Twist: yaad rakho

Ye sirf un AI tools ki photo pakadti hai jo SynthID nishaan lagate hain. Baaki AI tools ki photo bhi "not detected" aayegi, toh "not detected" asli ka saboot nahi hai.

Launch ke time 2–3 check ke baad "Something went wrong" aa sakta hai: thodi der ruk ke try karo.

7. "Not detected" aaye toh aur kya karein

  1. Google Lens se reverse search: photo pehle kahan aayi thi?
  2. Bhejne wale se poocho: original kahan se mili?
  3. Haath, ungliyan, likha hua text, background ke chehre dhyaan se dekho.

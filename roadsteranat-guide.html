3. Mera exact Roadster prompt (copy-paste)

/anatomy the new Tesla Roadster that jumped and flipped in the air at Tesla's October 2026 demo, with the side body cut away. Use only what Tesla said publicly and label the figure "Based on Tesla's own claims, not a full design". Show: the electric car body, the battery pack in the floor, a carbon-fibre tank holding about 600 litres of compressed air where the passenger seat would be, and 10 small cold-gas nozzles around the car (the same trick SpaceX uses on Falcon 9). Show the nozzles firing a burst of air so the car pushes off the ground and lifts a little. Interactive: a THRUST button, a live readout of thrust vs the car's weight (it only lifts when thrust is more than weight), a tank gauge that empties in about 5 seconds of thrust, and a note that a refill takes 2 to 6 hours. Use simple physics: weight = mass x 9.81, and the tank empties faster at higher thrust. Put every number you assume in a small "assumptions" box. Standalone HTML, dark theme.

Claude ne khud research karke 2 cheezein theek ki: 600 L = 400 L tank (passenger seat) + 200 L tank (seat ke peeche), aur 10 mein se 6 nozzle neeche (lift), 4 peeche (aage ka dhakka).

4. Bonus: pressure cooker prompt

/anatomy an Indian pressure cooker on a gas stove with its side cut away: water at the bottom, steam above it, the lid with a rubber gasket, the vent pipe with the weight (the whistle) sitting on it, and the small safety valve. Show the flame turning on, the pressure building, and the whistle lifting and venting steam when the pressure reaches about 1 bar (15 psi) at about 120 C. Interactive: a knob for the flame, a live readout of pressure and temperature, and a button to lift the whistle. Standalone HTML, dark theme.

5. Apna prompt kaise likho (formula)

  1. Machine + cut: "<machine> with its side cut away"
  2. Parts: jo andar dikhna chahiye, ek-ek naam se

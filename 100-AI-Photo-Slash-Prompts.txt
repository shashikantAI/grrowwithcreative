100 AI PHOTO SLASH PROMPTS
By @shashikant.ai

HOW TO USE
Upload your photo to an image-capable AI editor. Copy one slash prompt below and send it with your photo. Start each experiment from the original photo rather than stacking edits. Save the result you like.

These are short creative prompts, not official built-in commands. The slash is a convenient writing convention, not a guaranteed feature switch. Interpretation, face consistency and results vary by model and photo. Descriptions below explain the intended effect; you only need to copy the slash phrase to experiment.

[TRIED] means we generated and inspected a result using this exact short prompt in this project. It does not guarantee repeatability. All other entries are untested ideas. Some tried results differ from the initial concept: those actual results are described here.

KEEP EXPECTATIONS REAL
A short prompt can change your pose, outfit, background or face unexpectedly. Re-upload the original for each new attempt. If precision matters, add a plain-language instruction such as “Keep my face and outfit unchanged.” That is optional extra guidance, not part of the short prompts above.

ADS: how Instagram picks the one ad you see — 5 steps + the setting that actually matters

Free cheat-sheet for keyword ADS. Facts checked 1 Nov 2026 against Meta's own posts (sources at the bottom).

1. Crores of ads → 1 ad on your screen

1.All ads → crores

Who picks: advertisers + Meta AI tools

The pool keeps growing — Meta's AI tools now generate huge numbers of new ad versions.

2.Cut-off list (Andromeda) → a few thousand

Who picks: Andromeda AI

Not on this list = you will never see that ad. Meta: 'tens of millions of ad candidates into a few thousand'.

3.Expert AIs → ranked for YOU

Who picks: many 'user expert' models

Each ad is scored by the expert model that knows your taste best.

4.Ranking + auction → a handful

Who picks: bigger AI + bid + ad quality

Advertiser's bid AND ad quality decide the final pick — not money alone.

5.Your screen → 1

Who picks: you

Tap ⋯ → 'Why am I seeing this ad?' to see the main reasons.

The 100x news

Meta's ad-retrieval AI Andromeda (launched Dec 2024) was upgraded on 28 Oct 2026: about 100x more ads checked per second than the 2024 system (over one billion ads per second on one GPU server). That is throughput — it does not mean every single ad is picked 100x faster for you.

2. "See less" is weak

A 2024 study (Castleman & Korolova, AIES 2024) found that pressing "See less" did not significantly reduce ads on the 2 topics they tested (weight loss, parenting). It is one study, not proof that every control fails — but don't rely on it alone.

3. The setting that matters

Activity from other businesses → OFF

Instagram → profile → ☰ → Accounts Center → Ad preferences → Activity from other businesses → turn off "use this activity to show me ads".

Menu names can differ by app version and country — Meta is rolling this name out country by country (older apps may show "Your activity off Meta technologies"). One choice applies to all accounts in the same Accounts Center.

What it does — and does not do

✅ Meta stops using what other websites and apps tell it about you to pick your ads. In Meta's words: "If you don't, we won't use this information to show you more relevant ads or other content."

❌ Ads will NOT stop. You will still see the same number of ads — they just know less about your activity outside Instagram.

4. Two more quick checks

Ad topics: Accounts Center → Ad preferences → Ad topics → "See less" for topics you hate (weak, but free).

Why this ad: on any ad tap ⋯ → "Why am I seeing this ad?" — it shows the main signals used.

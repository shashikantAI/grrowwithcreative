Client delay email: the 4-part prompt (keyword EMAIL)

From House of Chaos, episode 1. Paste it in ChatGPT (or any AI chat), fill the brackets, send.

1. The prompt (fill the brackets)

Act as a [your role] at a [company type]. Write an email to our client [name] at [company]. Facts: delivery of [what] moves from [old date] to [new date] because [reason]; [what is already fixed or being done now]. Tone: honest and calm, no excuses, no over-apologising. Put the new date in the first two lines, say what we are doing so the date does not slip again, and offer a 10-minute call. Under 120 words, with a clear subject line. No placeholders.

Act as a project manager at a software company. Write an email to our client Mr. Mehta at Sunrise Retail.

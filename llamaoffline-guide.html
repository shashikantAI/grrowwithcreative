Free offline AI on your PC: which model to pick + setup (keyword OFFLINE)

My own test on my PC, 14 Nov 2026 (RTX 5070 Ti 16 GB, i7-14700F, 32 GB RAM). App + model info from llama.app (free, open source, by the llama.cpp team and Hugging Face).

1. Download the Llama app (free, no account)

Go to llama.app → Download for Windows 11 (Mac: Apple silicon, macOS 15+). It installs without admin rights.

2. Open it

Windows: click the Llama icon in the tray near the clock, or press Alt + Space.

3. Pick ONE model for your PC

ModelFileWho it is for
gemma-4 E4B4.59 GBStart here. My test model: 3–7 s per answer with a graphics card, about 2 min 20 s on CPU only.
gemma-4 12B7.22 GBBetter answers; llama.app's pick for 16 GB machines.
gpt-oss 20B12.1 GB24 GB RAM and up.
Qwen 3.8 27B19.0 GB32 GB RAM and up; strongest of the four.

Simple rule (my reading): the model file should fit in your graphics card memory for fast answers. If it only fits in normal RAM, it still works, just slowly. The RAM tiers above are llama.app's own (listed for Mac).

4. Click "Open chat" and ask

Write a polite leave email to my manager for tomorrow, 4 lines.
Mere boss ko Hinglish mein WhatsApp message likho ki main aaj 30 minute late aaunga, traffic ki wajah se.
Summarise this in 3 bullet points: (paste your text)

Honest notes

  • Internet is needed once: to download the app and the model. In my test the app also checked for updates when it started.
  • While answering: 954 connection checks in my test, 0 internet connections.
  • No graphics card = slow (1–3 minutes per answer on my CPU).
  • It is smaller than ChatGPT: great for daily small work, ChatGPT is still better for long, hard tasks.

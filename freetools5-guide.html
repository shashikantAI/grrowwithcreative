5 Free AI Tools (2 by Google): links, my example prompts, free limits

The tools from my reel, in the same order. Tested 2026-10-20 in Chrome. Free limits read from each official pricing/help page on 2026-10-20; they change often, so check before you rely on them.

1. Napkin AI — text to diagram

https://www.napkin.ai

My example (paste as one paragraph, then click the spark icon and pick a visual):

How a UPI payment reaches the shopkeeper in 2 seconds
You scan the QR code at the counter. Your bank app sends the request to NPCI. NPCI checks your account and the shop's account in the same second. The money moves, and both phones buzz with the confirmation. No cash, no card, no waiting.

Free: 500 AI credits per week (about 1 credit per word), unlimited editing, PNG/PDF export, Napkin branding on visuals. SVG/PPT export is paid (Plus, $9/month).

2. Krea — draw shapes, image updates live

https://www.krea.ai/realtime

Draw a sun, mountains and a house, then type:

Watercolor painting of a small village house under mountains at sunrise, soft warm light

Free: 100 compute units per day, refilled daily; realtime is available on free with limits.

3. Recraft — editable icons, SVG export

https://www.recraft.ai

Style: Minimal Line Icon → prompt:

a rocket launching, simple icon, two colours, white background

→ Vectorize → Export → Format SVG.

Free: daily credits (50/day per Recraft's own docs; my account showed 50+), 2 images per prompt. Free images are public and not licensed for commercial use. Whether SVG export is free is not stated officially (it worked for me).

4. Google Stitch — app idea to screens

https://stitch.withgoogle.com

My example:

A live cricket score app for India: home screen with the current IPL match as a big card, ball-by-ball commentary, dark theme with saffron accents

Free: free, no paid tier. Daily limits exist but are not stated on an official page.

5. Google Pomelli (by Google Labs) — on-brand marketing posts

https://labs.google/pomelli

Enter your website (I used talkspike.com) → it reads your colours, fonts and tagline → Create Campaigns → pick a suggestion → creatives.

Free: no charge, available in India (18+). Google says "several hundred" generations; I hit "daily creative generation limit" after about 2 campaigns.

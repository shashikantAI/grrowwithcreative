Your GTA 6 photo with free Gemini

My own test, 22 November 2026, on a free and a paid Gemini account.

1. Open Gemini

gemini.google.com or the Gemini app. A free Google account works.

2. Upload one clear photo of yourself

Face to the camera, good light, plain background works best.

3. Paste this prompt

Create an image: turn the person in the uploaded photo into an official Grand Theft Auto VI loading-screen artwork. Keep the face, hair and beard exactly as in the uploaded photo, do not restyle them. Painted comic-illustration style with bold ink outlines and cel shading, Vice City at sunset behind him: palm trees, neon pink and orange sky, a sports car. He wears a casual open shirt and sunglasses pushed up on his head, confident half-smile. Vertical 9:16 poster, no text, no logos.

For a woman, change "him" and "He" to "her" and "She". No beard? Delete "and beard".

4. Why these words

  • "Vertical 9:16": without it I got a square picture. 9:16 fits a story or a wallpaper.
  • "sunglasses pushed up on his head": without it the sunglasses sat on the eyes and hid the face.
  • "Keep the face … exactly as in the uploaded photo": keeps it looking like you.

5. Honest notes

  • Free account: the face held up; the picture came out smaller (768 x 1376 vs 1536 x 2752 on a paid account) and shop signs in the background had messy text.
  • It took about 20 seconds per picture in my test.
  • Gemini puts a small ✦ mark on the picture. Keep it.

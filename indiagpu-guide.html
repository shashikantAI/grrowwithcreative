Chip Design Roadmap: from zero to your first chip, free (keyword CHIP)

India already has about 1 in 5 of the world's chip design engineers (MeitY, Sept 2026) and the government's Chips to Startup programme wants to train 85,000 more. This is the order I would learn it in. Every link below was checked live on 2026-11-07. Learning is free at every step; the only paid items are marked PAID.

Step 0 — know the 3 jobs (2 minutes)

A chip team has three kinds of "recipe" engineers. Pick one later, start with the first.

RTL designYou write the chip's logic in Verilog / SystemVerilog. The "recipe".

Verification (DV)You prove the recipe has no bugs. Most open jobs in India are here.

Physical design (PD)You turn the logic into the actual layout that a fab like TSMC prints.

Step 1 — digital logic + Verilog (2–4 weeks, free)

1.HDLBits

https://hdlbits.01xz.net/wiki/Main_Page

180+ small Verilog problems, checked in the browser. Do 3 a day; nothing to install.

2.EDA Playground

https://www.edaplayground.com/

Run Verilog and a testbench in the browser with real simulators. Use it with HDLBits.

3.NPTEL: Digital IC Design (IIT Madras)

https://onlinecourses.nptel.ac.in/noc23_ee11/preview

Free to watch and learn. PAID only if you want the certificate exam (about ₹1,000).

4.Tiny Tapeout digital design lessons

https://tinytapeout.com/digital_design/

Beginner lessons from logic gates to a working design, written for people with zero background.

Step 2 — design a real (tiny) chip in the browser (1 weekend)

5.Tiny Tapeout + Wokwi template

https://wokwi.com/projects/354858054593504257

Draw a circuit with gates in the browser, simulate it, and it is already in the format a real chip shuttle accepts. Designing is free. PAID getting it printed on a shared chip (Tiny Tapeout shuttle slot, price on tinytapeout.com).

Step 3 — the real flow, RTL to GDS (2–3 months, free, open source)

This is the exact flow the reel calls "recipe → print": your Verilog becomes a layout file (GDS) a fab can manufacture.

6.NPTEL: VLSI Design Flow, RTL to GDS (IIIT Delhi)

https://onlinecourses.nptel.ac.in/noc24_ee102/preview

The whole pipeline in one course: synthesis, verification, physical design, testing. Free to learn.

7.OpenLane

https://github.com/The-OpenROAD-Project/OpenLane

Open-source tool that takes Verilog to GDS automatically. Run it on your own laptop.

8.SkyWater SKY130 PDK

https://github.com/google/skywater-pdk

A real, open 130 nm fab process kit. The same one Tiny Tapeout chips are made on.

9.Yosys

https://github.com/YosysHQ/yosys

The open-source synthesis engine inside OpenLane. Read its docs to understand what "synthesis" does.

10.OpenROAD flow scripts

https://github.com/The-OpenROAD-Project/OpenROAD-flow-scripts

Place-and-route flow with tutorials; this is the open version of what Cadence / Synopsys tools do.

Step 4 — build a CPU (1–2 months)

11.RISC-V MYTH workshop material

https://github.com/stevehoover/RISC-V_MYTH_Workshop

Build your own RISC-V CPU core step by step. The GitHub material is free; the guided cohort by VSD is PAID.

12.lowRISC Ibex

https://github.com/lowRISC/ibex

A small, production-quality open RISC-V core. Read the code after MYTH to see how industry writes RTL.

13.OpenHW CV32E40P

https://github.com/openhwgroup/cv32e40p

Another open 32-bit RISC-V core with a full verification setup, good for learning DV.

Step 5 — the government doors in India (free)

14.C2S registration (Chips to Startup, MeitY)

https://c2s.gov.in/form_loginindex_reg.jsp

Register as a student. Your college must be in the programme; 315 institutes are, 500 planned under India Semiconductor Mission 2.0.

15.C2S EDA tool support

https://c2s.gov.in/EDA_Tool_Support.jsp

Through C2S, students at enrolled colleges get the paid professional tools (Synopsys, Cadence, Siemens) free, plus fabrication of student designs at SCL Mohali.

16.C2S internships

https://c2s.gov.in/internship.jsp

Internship listings tied to the programme.

17.ChipIN Connect forum

https://chipinconnect.cdacb.in/

C-DAC's forum where students and chip startups in India ask questions and share designs.

18.India Semiconductor Mission

https://www.ism.gov.in/

Official site: schemes, approved fabs, and the design-linked incentive for startups.

Step 6 — when you are ready to go deeper (PAID, optional)

Zero to ASIC PAID

https://zerotoasiccourse.com/

The next step after Tiny Tapeout, ends in a real chip.

VLSI System Design (VSD) PAID

https://www.vlsisystemdesign.com/

Indian cohorts on OpenLane / SKY130 / RISC-V; some free install guides, workshops are paid.

The honest part

  • Nobody becomes a chip designer in 24 months of videos. HDLBits → Tiny Tapeout → OpenLane is about 6 months of evenings, and it gives you a GitHub you can show in an interview.
  • The 3 nm "kitchen" is not in India yet (Dholera starts at 90 nm / 28 nm). Design jobs are here; fabrication jobs mostly are not, yet.
  • Free means free to learn. Certificates, chip slots and cohorts cost money and none of them are required to get hired.

20 Free Tools to Make Videos with AI (DM for keyword TOOLS)

Every tool here is free to use, with its official GitHub link. The 5 marked “USED IN THE REEL” made the reel you just watched, on my own PC. Links checked 2026-10-31.

Motion + video

1.Remotion

https://github.com/remotion-dev/remotion

Make videos with React code: every scene, caption and animation is code, rendered to MP4. Free for individuals and small teams; bigger companies need a paid licence.

USED IN THE REEL

I put the Blender city into a Remotion scene and rendered the whole reel with it.

2.Remotion Skills

https://github.com/remotion-dev/skills

Official agent skills so Claude Code (and other AI agents) write correct Remotion code.

3.Anime.js

https://github.com/juliangarnier/anime

Small JavaScript animation library for smooth web and SVG animations.

4.Motion

https://github.com/motiondivision/motion

Animation library for React and JavaScript (formerly Framer Motion).

5.Manim

https://github.com/ManimCommunity/manim

Python engine for math and explainer animations (the 3Blue1Brown style).

USED IN THE REEL

I animated how the city was built: an 11 × 11 grid → 69 lots → taller near the centre.

Vector + 3D

6.Lottie

https://github.com/airbnb/lottie-web

Plays After Effects animations (exported as JSON) on the web, crisp at any size.

7.Blender MCP

https://github.com/ahujasid/mcp-for-blender

Connects Claude to Blender, so you describe a 3D scene in one line and Claude builds it.

USED IN THE REEL

One prompt → Claude built the 3D neon city in Blender (7 tries, it fixed its own mistakes).

8.Three.js

https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js

The standard library for 3D graphics in the browser.

9.three-gpu-pathtracer

https://github.com/gkjohnson/three-gpu-pathtracer

Realistic, ray-traced lighting for Three.js scenes, on the GPU.

10.Jolt Physics

https://github.com/jrouwe/JoltPhysics

Fast physics engine for falling, colliding and breaking objects.

VFX + AI + voice

11.postprocessing

https://github.com/pmndrs/postprocessing

Bloom, glow, depth of field and other film effects for Three.js.

12.three.quarks

https://github.com/Alchemist0823/three.quarks

Particle effects (fire, smoke, sparks) for Three.js.

13.Tweakpane

https://github.com/cocopon/tweakpane

Small control panel with sliders to tune animation values live.

14.ComfyUI

https://github.com/Comfy-Org/ComfyUI

Node-based app to run AI image and video models on your own computer.

USED IN THE REEL

I animated our city with the Wan 2.2 model, locally on my PC (5 s clip, about 6 minutes).

15.CosyVoice

https://github.com/QwenAudio/CosyVoice

Open-source text-to-speech and voice cloning model, supports many languages.

Sound + export

16.librosa

https://github.com/librosa/librosa

Python library for audio analysis: find the beats in music to cut on.

17.WhisperX

https://github.com/m-bain/whisperX

Speech to text with an exact timestamp for every word.

USED IN THE REEL

It gave me each word's timing, so the captions land exactly on my voice.

18.OBS Studio

https://github.com/obsproject/obs-studio

Free screen and camera recording.

19.LosslessCut

https://github.com/mifi/lossless-cut

Cut and trim video instantly without re-encoding (no quality loss).

20.FFmpeg

https://github.com/FFmpeg/FFmpeg

The command-line engine behind almost every video tool: convert, cut, mix, compress.

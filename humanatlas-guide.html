5 things to try

  1. Drag Explode anatomy to 100%: all 2,234 pieces separate.
  2. Tap 🔍, search aorta, femur or heart → tap the result → Isolate structure. The panel tells you what it does.
  3. Systems → keep only Skeleton, Muscles or Arteries on.
  4. The list says "Heart 23", but 5 of those are brain ventricles. The heart itself has 18 parts.
  5. NEET tip: rotate a structure once, close it, then draw it from memory.

Chrome ka Iceberg — 5 free Gemini tricks, step by step (shashikant.ai)

Tested on 4 Oct 2026 in Chrome on Windows (India). Reel mein jo dikha, wahi steps.

Pehle ek baar setup

  1. Chrome update karo (Menu → Help → About Google Chrome).
  2. Chrome mein apne Google account se sign in karo (Incognito mein kaam nahi karta).
  3. Top-right mein Ask Gemini button dabao. Pehli baar opt-in poochhe toh accept karo.
  4. Language Hindi bhi chalegi. Answer chahiye Hindi mein toh prompt ke end mein likho "in Hindi".

1. Summarize any page

Koi bhi lamba article kholo → Ask Gemini → type karo:

Summarize this page in 3 simple lines

Bonus: Make 5 quiz questions from this page · Explain this page like I am 15

2. Key points of any video

YouTube video play karo (Gemini sirf wahi video samajhta hai jo tumne chalaya hai) → Ask Gemini:

Give me the 3 main points of this video

Answer mein clickable timestamps aate hain — click karo, video wahin pahunch jaati hai.

3. Compare prices across tabs

Amazon aur Flipkart pe same product kholo (do alag tabs). Gemini box mein @ type karo → doosra tab select karo → poochho:

Where is iPhone 16 128GB cheaper, Amazon or Flipkart? Give prices.

Ek baar mein 10 tabs tak add kar sakte ho. Reel wale din: Flipkart ₹69,900 vs Amazon ₹86,900 (listing price; bank offers alag hote hain — checkout pe confirm karo).

4. New photo from any image

Jis page pe photo hai wahan Ask Gemini → type karo:

Edit the photo on this page: make it a night scene with fireworks in the sky

Gemini (Nano Banana) ek nayi photo banata hai — same jaisi, exact copy nahi.

5. Write emails without opening Gmail

Ask Gemini → type karo:

Write an email to <naam@email.com> that tomorrow's meeting is moved to 5 PM

Gemini Gmail ka draft bana deta hai: Send, Cancel ya Edit in Gmail. Send dabane se pehle padh lo.

Catch kya hai (honest)

  • Chrome mein sign in zaroori hai; 13+ age.
  • Google ka apna notice: Flash aur Pro models paid plans pe shift ho rahe hain; free mein Flash-Lite unlimited milta rahega. Matlab free = basic model.
  • "Auto-browse" (Gemini khud websites pe click kare) paid hai aur abhi sirf US mein.
  • AI galti kar sakta hai — prices aur facts ek baar khud check karo.

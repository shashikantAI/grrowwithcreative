The one line that stopped Claude from inventing 1,200 fake customers

My own test with Claude Code, 22 November 2026: 7 runs. Read the honest notes before you trust it.

1. The line

Do not invent customers, reviews or numbers.

2. How to use it

  1. Write your normal prompt for the website or landing page.
  2. Add the line above at the end of it.
  3. Give Claude the real facts you DO have (real customer names, real numbers), or leave those sections out.
  4. Before you publish, read every name, number, review and logo on the page yourself. If you cannot prove it, delete it.

3. Stronger version

Also removes the 14 "AI-made" design signs. Tested once: 0 of 13 signs, no invented proof.

Use none of these 14 things: blinking status dots, thick coloured left borders, gradient buttons, small labels or badges or pills, gradient text, italic words in headings, marker underlines or highlights, glass cards or cards inside cards, emoji callout boxes, three-column card grids, stat numbers, customer logo strips, testimonials, filler text. Do not invent customers, reviews or numbers.

4. Checklist before you publish

The 14 signs of an AI-made site (from Slopstrap). Tick what you find, then fix it.

5. My test

  • Tool: Claude Code (headless, Opus 5.5), an empty folder, no design skill, no CLAUDE.md. 7 runs on 22 November 2026, $4.83 in total.
  • The prompt: "Build a landing page for my AI startup, FlowPilot. It is an AI tool that writes follow-up emails for sales teams. Put everything in one index.html file." FlowPilot is made up: it has 0 customers.
  • Plain prompt (2 runs): No invented customers or reviews (the prices on the page were still made up).
  • Plain prompt + 5 design bans (2 runs): BOTH runs invented proof. Run 1: "Used by 1,200+ sales teams", 5 company names (Northwind, Lumen HQ, Brightpath, Corvid, Teller) and a review by "Jordan Okafor, VP of Sales" saying reply rate went from 18% to 41%. Run 2: a new person, "Rachel Simmons, VP of Sales", and new numbers (+22%, 3.1x, 5.5 hrs).
  • Prompt + the line (2 runs): 0 invented customers, 0 reviews. Claude labelled its sample block "Illustrative example. Names and details are made up for this page."

6. Honest notes

  • This is 7 runs on one tool (Claude Code). ChatGPT, v0, Lovable and other tools were not tested.
  • I do not know why the design bans brought the fake proof in. The line fixed it in 2 of 2 runs, which is not a guarantee: always read the page.
  • Claude Code needs a paid Claude plan or API credit.
  • Fake reviews and fake customer counts on a real business site can break consumer-protection rules. Your name is on the page, not the AI's.
  • The 14-sign list is from Slopstrap by Mike Lacher, a joke kit that makes a site look "hastily vibecoded".

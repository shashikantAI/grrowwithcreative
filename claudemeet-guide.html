Claude Meetings: setup steps + the 3 commands + privacy switches (keyword MEET)

Source: Anthropic's help page Use Claude Meetings (read 2026-11-13). I have not tested it myself yet: it needs a Claude Team or Enterprise plan. The 3 commands are the ones used in Anthropic's own launch demo.

Google Meet ✓Microsoft Teams: not yetZoom: not yetTeam + Enterprise plans (beta)

1. Owner: turn on Google Calendar for the organisation

Team plan: Claude Meetings is already on. Enterprise: Organization settings > Products > Claude Meetings (beta)

2. You: open Meetings in Claude and connect your Google Calendar

3. Pick the meeting and click "Add Claude"

Optional auto-join: Settings > Capabilities > Meetings > Auto-join (all meetings, or all except one-on-ones).

4. In the call, say "Claude" and then the request (or type it in the Meet chat)

Hey Claude, can you start a whiteboard, please?
Claude, sort these from easiest to hardest to produce.
Ok Claude, organize next steps to get this launched by the holidays.

Also: "Claude, make a doc" · "Claude, stop listening" · "Claude, leave".

5. Privacy switches to check first

  • Ask everyone first: Anthropic says make sure everyone on the call is okay with being transcribed.
  • Saved: transcript, summary and screen descriptions. Audio is not saved.
  • Outside guests: Team = Claude can join by default (owner can turn it off). Enterprise = off until the owner allows it. Your own switch: Settings > Capabilities > Meetings > Meetings with external guests
  • Remove it any time: say "Claude, leave" or press "Remove Claude" on the meeting page.

After the call: one meeting page with the summary, action items, transcript and everything Claude made. Cost (2026-11-13): notes and transcripts are included; docs and whiteboards made in a meeting are free through 10 Feb 2027.

How to use the prompt

  1. Copy the prompt below.
  2. Change the brand name, city, colours, menu items and prices to yours.
  3. Paste it into ChatGPT or Claude. In the reel, ChatGPT (Work mode) built the site in 9 minutes 34 seconds.
  4. Open the HTML file it gives you in your browser. If something looks off, tell the AI exactly which section to fix.
Tip: swap any reference for one you found yourself. "NAVBAR (like navbar.gallery)" can become "NAVBAR like the Supaste navbar on navbar.gallery: a floating pill…".

The prompt

Build a landing page for my chai brand called Kulhad (Pune). It must look like an award-winning website
(Awwwards / Apple product page level), not a template. Single HTML file.

Style references I studied, use these ideas:

1. HERO (like the best sites on recent.design): full-screen, near-black warm background (#0E0B09) with film grain.
   A giant headline across the whole width, "CHAI, BUT CINEMATIC." in a heavy condensed font, letters revealing
   one by one from below with a mask. In the middle, a 3D-looking clay kulhad cup built with CSS/SVG
   (terracotta gradients, highlights, rim shadow) that slowly rotates and floats, with animated steam wisps rising
   (SVG paths + blur). The cup tilts slightly toward the mouse.
2. NAVBAR (like navbar.gallery): a floating glass pill at the top, blurred, that shrinks when you scroll.
   Logo left, 3 links, one glowing "Order now" button right.
3. MOTION (like 60fps.design): buttery micro-interactions. A custom round cursor that grows over buttons,
   magnetic buttons that pull toward the cursor, a press-down on click, sections that fade and slide up with
   stagger as you scroll, numbers that count up.
4. SCROLL STORY (like loadmo.re, bold and not boring): a sticky section where, as you scroll, the cup stays pinned
   and 3 big steps change beside it: "01 Crush the spices" → "02 Simmer 12 minutes" → "03 Pour into clay".
   The background colour shifts from black to deep terracotta to cream as you scroll through it.
5. MARQUEE: a huge infinite scrolling text band "MASALA · ADRAK · ELAICHI · KULHAD ·" in outline letters.
6. MENU (like posts.design): 3 cards styled like premium brand posts, each with a bold price as the hero (₹45, ₹60,
   ₹80), a rich gradient background and a 3D tilt on hover.
7. CTA (like cta.gallery): a final full-width block with a massive line "Your first cup is on us." and one big
   magnetic button "Claim free chai →".

Rules: 2 fonts max (one heavy condensed display font, one clean sans), terracotta #C8553D + cream #F4E9DA + black,
everything animates smoothly at 60fps with transform/opacity only, respects prefers-reduced-motion, looks great on
a phone too. No stock photos, no emoji, no lorem ipsum, no fake reviews or ratings.
"Kulhad" is a demo brand made for the reel. Results vary with the AI model and plan you use.

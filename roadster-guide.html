Apne aap ko Elon ke flying Roadster mein daalo — free, 3 steps (shashikant.ai)

Tested on 16 Oct 2026 on Windows (India). Reel mein jo dikha, wahi steps. Paisa zero.

Step 1 — Apni photo se 2 stills banao (Gemini, free)

gemini.google.com kholo → "+" → Upload files → apni ek front-facing photo (saaf chehra, plain background best).

Phir yeh prompt paste karo (English mein hi rakho, AI English prompt better samajhta hai):

Create a photorealistic image. Use the uploaded photo as the exact identity reference: this same man, face, hair and beard exactly as in the photo, do not restyle him. He is sitting in the driver's seat of a white 2026 Tesla Roadster, black t-shirt, hands on the steering wheel, confident smirk, looking slightly ahead. Camera is inside the car at a three-quarter angle from the passenger side so his face is clearly visible. Through the windshield and side window: a sunny Indian highway toll plaza with yellow-and-black striped booths, an overhead sign that reads FASTag, and a queue of cars waiting ahead. Cinematic daylight, shallow depth of field, 16:9 landscape.

Doosra prompt (bahar ka shot, isme chehra nahi chahiye):

Now create a second photorealistic image of the same scene from outside the car: a white 2026 Tesla Roadster seen from behind at a high rear three-quarter angle, slowing down on an Indian highway as it approaches the same FASTag toll plaza with yellow-and-black striped booths, a queue of cars in the lanes ahead, sunny daylight, wide cinematic shot, 16:9 landscape, no people visible.

Dono images download karo. Tip: apna chehra kabhi words mein describe mat karo ("curly hair" etc.) — sirf "exactly as in the photo" likho, warna AI chehra badal deta hai.

Har shot = ek image + ek prompt. Yeh 4 prompts copy karo:

Shot 1 — andar, button (interior still, 3–4 s)

The man in the driver's seat glances at the toll plaza ahead, smirks, then reaches down with his right hand and presses a red button on the centre console. His face, hair and beard stay exactly the same. Subtle handheld camera, realistic lighting, photorealistic.

Shot 2 — rocket jump (exterior still, 5 s)

The white sports car suddenly fires rocket thrusters: a burst of white smoke and flame erupts from under the rear, the car launches steeply into the air and flies up and over the toll booths, leaving a white smoke trail. The camera tilts up to follow the car. Realistic physics, dramatic, cinematic, photorealistic.

Shot 3 — landing (Shot 2 ka LAST frame screenshot karke image banao, 5 s)

The white sports car in the sky descends with its smoke trail, lands hard on the empty highway beyond the toll plaza with a small bounce and a puff of smoke, then speeds away down the road. Aerial drone camera follows it down. Realistic physics, cinematic, photorealistic.

Shot 4 — hasna (interior still, 3–4 s)

The man in the driver's seat bursts out laughing, throws his head back, claps his hands once, then grins straight at the camera. His face, hair and beard stay exactly the same. Subtle handheld camera, realistic lighting, photorealistic.

Trick: Shot 3 ko Shot 2 ke last frame se shuru karo — car aur smoke same rehte hain, cut dikhta nahi.

Step 3 — Jodo aur sound dalo (CapCut, phone pe)

  1. Order: bahar wala still (1.5 s zoom) → Shot 1 → Shot 2 → Shot 3 → Shot 4.
  2. Sound: button pe "click", launch pe "whoosh", landing pe "impact", end pe "crowd wow" — sab CapCut ke free sound library mein hain. Niche ek halki music bed.
  3. Export 1080×1920. Text sirf last 2 second mein.

Catch kya hai (honest)

  • Free = 480p–720p aur kabhi watermark. 1080p ke liye Kling/Dreamina ka paid plan.
  • Chehra 2–3 clips mein thoda drift karta hai — jo clip galat lage, dobara generate karo (seed badlo).
  • Kisi aur ka chehra bina permission use mat karo — platforms ki policy hai aur India ke IT rules bhi.
  • Real Roadster abhi udta nahi, demo tha; yeh sirf AI masti hai.

Part 2 — Challan wala version (Google Flow, free) — 18 Oct 2026 reel

Yeh wala reel (police rokti hai → "Aur kuch?" → button → car upar) Google Flow pe bana, 2 clips, 20 credits, paisa zero.

Flow setup

flow.google.com (Google login; free account = 50 credits/day, Veo 3.1 Lite = 10 credits per 8 s clip). Project kholo → prompt box mein model ke naam pe click → Video → Frames → 9:16. Ab "Start" aur "End" frame ke do slot dikhenge. Image upload "+" → Upload media se.

Clip A — police stop (Start frame → End frame)

  • Start frame (F1): Gemini / ChatGPT mein apni photo attach karke yeh image banao (prompt English mein):
Photorealistic night photo from a traffic policeman's chest-mounted body camera: the uploaded photo is the exact identity reference, same man, face, hair and beard exactly as in the photo, black t-shirt. He sits in the driver's seat of a white 2026 Tesla Roadster, driver window down, looking up at the camera, both hands on the wheel, a glowing red button on the centre console. Behind the car a yellow-and-black MUMBAI TRAFFIC POLICE barricade, wet asphalt reflecting orange streetlights, autos passing far away. A khaki police sleeve and a handheld e-challan device at the bottom edge of the frame. Portrait 9:16.
  • End frame (F2): wahi image dobara edit karo, sirf ek cheez badlo:
Edit this photo. Keep everything exactly the same. Change only one thing: his right hand has left the steering wheel and rests on the centre console with his thumb on top of the glowing red button, about to press it. Left hand stays on the wheel. Face completely deadpan, mouth closed. Portrait 9:16.
  • F1 ko Start slot, F2 ko End slot mein daalo, phir yeh prompt:
Continuous handheld police body-camera POV at chest height, night, slight natural shake, no cuts, no camera moves. The man in the driver's seat is the only person who speaks. [00:00-00:01] A policeman's fist in a khaki sleeve enters from the bottom edge and knocks twice on the roof edge above the window. SFX: two knocks. [00:01-00:04] The man looks up at the camera, bored, mouth closed, waits in silence. [00:04-00:05] He says in Hindi, flat and deadpan, barely moving: "Aur kuch?" [00:05-00:08] He holds eye contact in silence, then slowly moves his right hand off the steering wheel down to the centre console and rests his thumb on the glowing red button, settling exactly into the final frame. No other dialogue, the policeman is never visible. Ambient noise: distant traffic, light rain. Photorealistic raw bodycam footage, no on-screen text, no subtitles, no music. Only one car in frame, the car does not move.

Galti jo maine ki: police ki lines prompt mein mat likho. Frame mein sirf tumhara chehra hai, toh Veo saari lines tumhare muh se bulwa deta hai. Police ki awaaz baad mein edit mein dalo (koi bhi Hindi TTS, ya Flow ka pehla clip jisme awaaz aayi ho).

Clip B — launch (Clip A ka last frame → sky frame)

  • Start frame: Clip A ka last frame (video pause karke screenshot, ya Flow mein "+" se frame save karo).
  • End frame (F3): usi last frame ko ChatGPT/Gemini mein edit karo:
New version of the same moment, but the body-camera has tilted straight up to follow the car. The same white sports car is about 25 metres up in the black Mumbai night sky, seen from directly below: underside and rear visible, two blazing blue-white cold-gas thruster jets firing straight down, long plumes of white vapour streaming down and filling the lower half of the frame. A hint of orange streetlight glow and the top edge of the yellow MUMBAI TRAFFIC POLICE barricade at the very bottom. No people, no second car, no text. Photorealistic bodycam footage, slight motion blur, portrait 9:16.
  • Prompt:
Continuous handheld police body-camera POV, night, no cuts. The same white sports car and the same man as the first frame. [00:00-00:01] The man presses the red button with his thumb; a deep hiss builds, a pale blue-white glow spreads under the car. [00:01-00:03] Pressurised cold-gas thrusters under the car fire: bright blue-white jets, no fire, no flame; a wall of white vapour erupts and the whole car, with the man inside it, lifts straight up and rises out of the top of the frame. The road where it stood is now empty wet asphalt. [00:03-00:06] The camera tilts up fast to follow it: through thinning vapour the same white car climbs vertically on two blue-white jets into the black sky, seen from directly below. [00:06-00:08] The camera settles looking straight up at the car hanging high above on its two jets, vapour plumes filling the lower frame, the top of the yellow police barricade at the bottom edge, exactly the final frame. SFX: button click, deep hiss, a roaring rush of high-pressure gas, softening as the car climbs. No speech, no music, no on-screen text. Only ONE car exists in the whole clip; nothing remains parked on the road. Four normal upright tyres, the car never turns around, the policeman is never visible. Photorealistic raw bodycam footage.

Sabse badi galti: Flow ka Extend button launch ke liye mat use karo — meri pehli try mein ek car ud gayi aur doosri car neeche khadi rahi. Start frame + End frame wala tarika hi chalta hai: end frame mein car aasmaan mein hai, toh neeche car bach hi nahi sakti.

Edit (CapCut / koi bhi editor)

  1. Clip A ko 7 s pe kaato, Clip B ko 0.9 s se shuru karo — button press cut pe hi padta hai, stare lamba nahi lagta.
  2. Police ki 2 lines (License RC PUC dikhao / Insurance aur Aadhaar bhi) 0.7 s aur 4.5 s pe; "Aur kuch?" 3.0 s pe (jahan muh khulta hai).
  3. Subtitles neeche, music: stop ke time halka suspense, launch pe loud. Last 2 s mein thoda zoom-in taaki barricade ka text (jo AI mein bigad jaata hai) frame se bahar chala jaye.
  4. Post karte waqt "AI-generated" label ON.

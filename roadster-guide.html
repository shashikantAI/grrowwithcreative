Apne aap ko Elon ke flying Roadster mein daalo — free, 3 steps (shashikant.ai)

Tested on 16 Oct 2026 on Windows (India). Reel mein jo dikha, wahi steps. Paisa zero.

Step 1 — Apni photo se 2 stills banao (Gemini, free)

gemini.google.com kholo → "+" → Upload files → apni ek front-facing photo (saaf chehra, plain background best).

Phir yeh prompt paste karo (English mein hi rakho, AI English prompt better samajhta hai):

Create a photorealistic image. Use the uploaded photo as the exact identity reference: this same man, face, hair and beard exactly as in the photo, do not restyle him. He is sitting in the driver's seat of a white 2026 Tesla Roadster, black t-shirt, hands on the steering wheel, confident smirk, looking slightly ahead. Camera is inside the car at a three-quarter angle from the passenger side so his face is clearly visible. Through the windshield and side window: a sunny Indian highway toll plaza with yellow-and-black striped booths, an overhead sign that reads FASTag, and a queue of cars waiting ahead. Cinematic daylight, shallow depth of field, 16:9 landscape.

Doosra prompt (bahar ka shot, isme chehra nahi chahiye):

Now create a second photorealistic image of the same scene from outside the car: a white 2026 Tesla Roadster seen from behind at a high rear three-quarter angle, slowing down on an Indian highway as it approaches the same FASTag toll plaza with yellow-and-black striped booths, a queue of cars in the lanes ahead, sunny daylight, wide cinematic shot, 16:9 landscape, no people visible.

Dono images download karo. Tip: apna chehra kabhi words mein describe mat karo ("curly hair" etc.) — sirf "exactly as in the photo" likho, warna AI chehra badal deta hai.

Har shot = ek image + ek prompt. Yeh 4 prompts copy karo:

Shot 1 — andar, button (interior still, 3–4 s)

The man in the driver's seat glances at the toll plaza ahead, smirks, then reaches down with his right hand and presses a red button on the centre console. His face, hair and beard stay exactly the same. Subtle handheld camera, realistic lighting, photorealistic.

Shot 2 — rocket jump (exterior still, 5 s)

The white sports car suddenly fires rocket thrusters: a burst of white smoke and flame erupts from under the rear, the car launches steeply into the air and flies up and over the toll booths, leaving a white smoke trail. The camera tilts up to follow the car. Realistic physics, dramatic, cinematic, photorealistic.

Shot 3 — landing (Shot 2 ka LAST frame screenshot karke image banao, 5 s)

The white sports car in the sky descends with its smoke trail, lands hard on the empty highway beyond the toll plaza with a small bounce and a puff of smoke, then speeds away down the road. Aerial drone camera follows it down. Realistic physics, cinematic, photorealistic.

Shot 4 — hasna (interior still, 3–4 s)

The man in the driver's seat bursts out laughing, throws his head back, claps his hands once, then grins straight at the camera. His face, hair and beard stay exactly the same. Subtle handheld camera, realistic lighting, photorealistic.

Trick: Shot 3 ko Shot 2 ke last frame se shuru karo — car aur smoke same rehte hain, cut dikhta nahi.

Step 3 — Jodo aur sound dalo (CapCut, phone pe)

  1. Order: bahar wala still (1.5 s zoom) → Shot 1 → Shot 2 → Shot 3 → Shot 4.
  2. Sound: button pe "click", launch pe "whoosh", landing pe "impact", end pe "crowd wow" — sab CapCut ke free sound library mein hain. Niche ek halki music bed.
  3. Export 1080×1920. Text sirf last 2 second mein.

Catch kya hai (honest)

  • Free = 480p–720p aur kabhi watermark. 1080p ke liye Kling/Dreamina ka paid plan.
  • Chehra 2–3 clips mein thoda drift karta hai — jo clip galat lage, dobara generate karo (seed badlo).
  • Kisi aur ka chehra bina permission use mat karo — platforms ki policy hai aur India ke IT rules bhi.
  • Real Roadster abhi udta nahi, demo tha; yeh sirf AI masti hai.

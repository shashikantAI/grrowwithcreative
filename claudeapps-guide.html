The 26-word prompt that made my desktop app with Claude + 5 app ideas

My own test on Claude Desktop, 26 November 2026.

Steps

1Open the Claude Desktop app on Mac or Windows and sign in.
2Paste this prompt and send it.
Build me a monthly expense tracker app. I add an expense in rupees with a category, and it shows this month's total and a category chart.
3When the app appears, click its name at the top, then Install as app, then Install.
4Open it from the new icon on your desktop (on Windows it is also in the Start menu under Claude Apps).

5 more app ideas (paste any one as the prompt)

1Udhaar khata
Build me an udhaar tracker app. I add a person's name and the amount in rupees I gave or took, and it shows who owes me how much.
2EMI calculator
Build me an EMI calculator app. I enter loan amount in rupees, interest rate and months, and it shows the monthly EMI and total interest.
3Habit streaks
Build me a habit tracker app. I add daily habits and tick them each day, and it shows my streak for every habit this week.
4Trip split
Build me a trip expense split app. I add friends and who paid what in rupees, and it shows who has to pay whom.
5Focus timer
Build me a 25-minute focus timer app with a 5-minute break, and show how many sessions I finished today.

Good to know

  • My test: the expense tracker was ready in under 2 minutes, and its data was still there after I closed and reopened it.
  • The 5 ideas are ideas in the same style. I have not tested them yet.
  • Install as app is in beta. Anthropic says it is rolling out on Free and paid plans; I tested on a paid plan.
  • You need internet and a signed-in Claude Desktop to open the app.
  • Building an app, and any request the app sends to Claude, counts toward your plan's usage limit.
  • If an app asks for "Full access to this computer", allow it only when you really need it and trust the app: it can read, change or delete your files.
